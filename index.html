    What it is

    Blueprint

    The referral graph for the creative industry

    A referral-only platform where brands and creative professionals discover, verify, and hire trusted collaborators — faster. Built for India's creative economy, where the best work already moves through references.

    Who we are

    The trust layer between brands and creative talent

    • A network where brands, founders, and creative professionals meet through people they already trust
    • Referral-only by design — every member enters through a reference, so quality and accountability are built in
    • Positioned at the intersection of India's creator economy, gig workforce, e-commerce, and D2C growth
    • Our thesis: formalize the reference economy — turn informal network effects into market infrastructure

    The problem

    Creative hiring still runs on fragmented trust

    • Hiring happens through Instagram DMs, WhatsApp groups, and word-of-mouth
    • Teams rebuild local networks from scratch in every new city
    • Great talent outside major hubs stays hard to discover
    • Trust exists — but the infrastructure does not

    How a brand hires today

    Need talent→ DM groups→ Ask friends→ Cold outreach→ Uncertain quality→ Slow assembly
    Our solution & what makes it unique

    Blueprint turns informal referrals into searchable infrastructure

    How it works

    1. Register as Professional, Brand/Founder, or both
    2. Post needs by city + category
    3. Notify relevant, trusted people instantly
    4. Discover work through a relevance-first feed
    5. Search a structured professional directory

    What makes it unique

    Referral-only entryEvery member vouched for · referrer stays visible
    City + category matchingLocal, workflow-native discovery — not a global open marketplace
    Trust as the productBetter signal than open platforms · curation for premium work
